# Glass-v2 pages

What each page of the panel's design shows and does, and how to run it in the simulator. Moved from the README on 2026-10-30.

`styles/glass-v2` with `jarvis_orb.py` is what `board.py` builds for the board (see `docs/voice-plan.md`, Build order). Since 2026-10-22 it has five pages:

- **Gå hemifrån**, the dashboard. The clock and date are live, and so are the Jarvis card's bubbles (`sensor.jarvis_chat`). The latest question and reply remain complete inside a vertically scrollable chat; drag there to read the whole text. New text follows the bottom only while you are already there. They only show messages from the last 10 minutes, so a guest does not read old questions. While no bubble shows, the card shows **Härnäst** instead (2026-11-02): at most five lines of what is coming that the other cards do not show, from `sensor.jarvis_hemmet`'s `next_up` (`server/agents/home_status.py` `next_up`): a problem at home (the door open, bad air), an outage or planned work from Ellevio, Exergi or Stockholm Vatten over home that runs or starts within 24 hours (the house line below), reminders due within 12 hours, the laundry slot when it starts within 36 hours or runs now, and from 18:00 the next wakeup. Nothing coming leaves the card as before. **Rensa** empties them and calls `jarvis_agent.clear`, so Jarvis forgets the conversation. Pills over the clock show the running timer and, in red, when Jarvis has no internet or the panel has lost Home Assistant. Since 2026-10-22 the cards are live too (`docs/readme/dashboard.jpg`): the weather from SMHI with an icon (Material Design Icons glyphs) and the next three days under a thin line; a tap on the card opens the weather page (`styles/glass-v2/weather_page.py`, data from `server/agents/weather.py` `panel()`): now with wind, humidity, rain chance and pressure, the next 24 hours as a curve with rain bars, and seven days with rain and gusts (design: `sketch/weather_mock.py`), the next three events today (tomorrow's once today is over; a day with one or two left is filled with tomorrow's, their time "i morgon 09:30"; when today and tomorrow are both empty, the week's next three under **KOMMANDE**, "tors 18:00" or "tors heldag", from the calendar page's seven-day read, 2026-11-02) with the bar coloured by calendar, and the metro from Stadshagen, two trains towards the city and two the other way, with "gå nu" counted from the walk to the platform (`JARVIS_WALK_MIN` in `server.env`, 3 by default). SL's disruptions take the fourth row in orange (`sl_note`, `home_status.metro_note`, 2026-11-02): Stadshagen's own notices, the shown trains' deviations and the blue line's messages from SL's deviations API (asked on the SL worker at most every 5 minutes, only after a good departures reply); lifts, escalators and other stations' entrances are left out. The journal logs when the line comes and goes (`SL disruption line on/off`) and each change in the blue line's message count.
- **Kalender** (`styles/glass-v2/calendar_page.py`, since 2026-10-30): a tap on the dashboard's calendar card opens the next seven days. Today and tomorrow are on the left (up to six rows each), the five days after on the right (ten rows together); all-day events first, coloured by calendar, events that are over dimmed, and "+N till" when a day is fuller than its rows. A tap on an event shows it whole over the page: day and time, the full title and its calendar; a tap closes it (2026-11-02). Since 2026-11-04 the card also shows what iCloud holds about the event, each row with an icon and hidden when empty: its alarms as the times they ring (for a timed event also how long before), place, how it repeats, notes (cut to five lines) and link; "Inga påminnelser" when it has none. Jarvis reads them with the calendar MCP's `show_event`, one call per event after each week read. When the last read failed and older events show, the corner says "Kunde inte uppdatera · senast 14:05" in orange instead of the week number. `server/agents/calendar_week.py` reads `agenda(days=7)` on its own MCP session, again when the sync's revision changes and otherwise every five minutes, and packs the rows as `cal_week`. A tap anywhere goes back.
- **Påminnelser och timers** (`styles/glass-v2/reminders_page.py`, since 2026-11-03): a tap on Hemmet's "Påminnelser och timers" card (not on its quick-timer buttons) opens every running timer and every pending reminder. Left: up to four timers, shortest first, each with its name, the time left in orange, **+1 min** and **Avbryt**, and **5 min**, **10 min**, **15 min** under them. Right: up to nine reminders, soonest first (a delivery Jarvis could not confirm has its time in orange), and "+N till" past them. A tap on a reminder shows it whole over the page with **Ta bort** and **Stäng**; Ta bort dims the row until Home Assistant's next `rem_list`, which follows at once. Reminders are added by voice only. The reminders are `sensor.jarvis_hemmet`'s `rem_list` (`server/reminders.py` `panel_rows`, ids included); the timers are the panel's own Assist timers, filled on every tick by `board_config/voice.py` (`reminders_page.TIMER_ROWS`). Ta bort, +1 min and a single Avbryt send the exact id with `reminders_view.h`'s `call()` (the JSON config cannot give `homeassistant.action` a lambda's value, and picking by position could hit another row when the list changed): `jarvis_agent.reminder_cancel` (POST `/reminders/cancel` on Jarvis), `jarvis_agent.timer_add` and `jarvis_agent.timer_cancel` with `timer_id`, which act only on the panel's own timers. Logs: the panel's `reminders` tag (`Ta bort: reminder N`, `... sent`, `not sent: no Home Assistant`), HA's `Panel reminder N: cancelled`/`Panel timer ...` lines, and the server journal's `Reminder N cancelled from the panel`. A tap anywhere else goes back.
- **Hemmet** (`docs/readme/hemmet.jpg`): `sensor.jarvis_hemmet` on the dashboard's glass: a green check and "Allt lugnt hemma." (an orange "!" and the problem when something is wrong) with the **house line** under it (2026-11-02, `house`, `home_status.house_line`): in orange an outage or planned work from Joel's own providers (Ellevio through Strömkoll, Stockholm Exergi, Stockholm Vatten: `PROVIDERS`) whose area polygon covers `zone.home`, running now or starting within 7 days, as "Ellevio: strömavbrott pågår, till ca 23:00", from Följdfel's collector database on Jarvis (`~/foljdfel/data/foljdfel.sqlite`, read-only, `JARVIS_FOLJDFEL_DB`; the journal logs `provider disruptions over home: N` on each change and any read failure). Not a radius: Ellevio alone lists ~25 outages a day around Stockholm, and over Följdfel's first three weeks (12 Oct–2 Nov) the area rule matched 2 Ellevio outages, hidden when there is none (the BRF's news were shown there too for an hour and removed as bloat), inside, the alarms (dimmed while all is well), reminders and timers (with **5 min**, **10 min** and **15 min** while none runs and **Avbryt** while one does, 2026-11-02: Home Assistant's own timers on the panel through `jarvis_agent.timer_start` and `timer_cancel`, `ha/custom_components/jarvis_agent/panel_timers.py`, the same as asked by voice; a refusal is a WARN line in the panel log and the reason in HA's log), the laundry slot and the sun. Each card has a small white icon (`bake.py`'s `draw_more_icons`). On smoke or a leak the panel goes there at full brightness, at night too.
- **Tavla** (`docs/readme/tavla.jpg`): the day's picture alone, without glass, with a small plaque for its title and who made it (`art_title`, `art_credit` from `sensor.jarvis_hemmet`). A tap on the plaque opens NASA's English explanation; **Jorden** (top right) shows NASA EPIC's newest whole Earth and today's closest asteroids ([docs/nasa-refresh.md](nasa-refresh.md#tavlas-earth-view-and-explanation)). A tap anywhere else goes back to the dashboard.

**The background** is chosen in Home Assistant with **Jarvis bakgrund** (`select.jarvis_bakgrund`): NASA's picture of the day (the default), Växlande, Stjärnenatten, Mandelblom, four daily pictures from free APIs, any picture you upload, or Egen bild from a link. Jarvis copies the chosen picture to `/local/hallpanel` at once (`server/agents/background.py`), as `bg.png` with the glass and `art.png` without it, and the panel fetches it a few seconds later (it follows `bg_version` in `sensor.jarvis_hemmet`). The 07:30 NASA run keeps any other choice.

- **Daily pictures** (2026-11-02, `server/daily_pictures.py`, `hallpanel-daily-pictures.timer` every three hours): **Dagens målning** (a CC0 painting from the Cleveland Museum of Art, landscape, not one of the last 120), **Dagens foto** (Wikimedia's featured picture; a portrait day keeps the last), **Bing** (Bing's photo of the day) and **NASA Jordbild** (NASA Earth Observatory's image of the day, weekdays; its maps often carry place names). Each keeps only its latest picture, at most 1920 px wide, in `~/.local/share/hallpanel/daily/<slug>` (about 3 MB for all four); a picture that looks like one any source kept before (dHash, `seen.json`) is skipped. **Växlande** shows another of NASA and these four every Stockholm day, skipping a picture it has shown; the timer's 00:50 run turns it. Inställningar's Bakgrund list holds the first 8 choices, so an uploaded picture after them is chosen in Home Assistant. Log: `journalctl --user-unit hallpanel-daily-pictures`, one line per source and run. Tests: `tests/test_daily_pictures.py`. The Art Institute of Chicago's image server answered 403 to the honest User-Agent, so it is not a source; trying other User-Agents would get around its bot check, so don't.
- **Upload**: Media in Home Assistant's side menu > **hallpanel-bilder** > upload (jpg, png or webp; an iPhone's HEIC does not work). Within about 10 s the picture is a choice in Jarvis bakgrund, named after its file. Deleting it there makes the background NASA again.
- **Link**: paste a picture's address in **Jarvis bakgrund länk** (`text.jarvis_bakgrund_lank`, at most 255 characters). Jarvis downloads it (at most 25 MB) and it becomes **Egen bild**; a link that is not a picture shows Jarvis's reason in Home Assistant.
- The glass is darker since 2026-10-22 (`GLASS_TARGET`, `GLASS_MIN`, `GLASS_MAX` and `GLASS_SPOT` in `bake.py`): white text against the brightest spots in the cards went from 4.1–8.0 to 6.7–10.8 in contrast on the galaxy, Stjärnenatten and Mandelblom.
- A new picture is cropped to 1024x600 (a portrait photo loses much at the top and bottom) and the glass baked into it on Jarvis, in a separate Python process with a small Rust sampler, then kept. Glass geometry is computed only around each widget; the [cold image experiment](baseline/image-experiment-2026-10-25.md) compares CPU time, peak memory and every output pixel with the original renderer. The checked deploy builds and tests Rust on Jarvis before installing it.

**The dashboard Hallpanel** in Home Assistant's side menu (`/jarvis-hallpanel`, made by `ha/hallpanel_dashboard.py`) has all of this in one place: the background and the link, the panel's night times, Nattljus, Skärmljus and Mikrofon (unavailable until the panel is adopted), and Rensa.

**Inställningar** (`docs/readme/settings.jpg`) opens from the cog at the end of the tab bar, on the dashboard and Hemmet, as a sheet over a dimmed screen: **Skärmljus** (the day's level, kept on the panel and set again after night mode; a change in Home Assistant by day is kept too), **Nattljus** (the screen shows the level while the slider moves), **Volym** (the listening chime plays at the new level), **Mikrofon** (the same switch as the long press) and **Bakgrund** (a list of the first 8 choices in Jarvis bakgrund; the panel fetches the picture a few seconds later, and a pick closes the sheet once its picture is on screen, so Klar is not needed: `bg_shown` in gen.py, only within 60 s of the pick, logged as `Settings: closed, the picked background is in`). A tap beside the sheet, Klar, Natt, an alarm or the voice page closes it. In the simulator the sliders only log: `python3 jarvis_orb.py --page installningar` opens on it.

**Mikrofon** is a switch on the panel's device page in Home Assistant, kept on the panel. Off stops the wake word, for guests, a phone call or a TV that wakes Jarvis. **The mic button** on the panel (the round one by the tab bar; the Jarvis card's small one and its "Säg hey Jarvis" line went on 2026-11-02 so the chat and Härnäst have its rows) does the same by hand: a tap talks to Jarvis without the wake word (or stops him while he listens or talks), and a long press turns the microphone off or on. While it is off it is red with a slashed mic and a tap only blinks it; there is no pill over the clock, since the red button already shows it (`docs/readme/settings.jpg`). Inställningar has the same switch, in sight of guests too (Joel's choice, 2026-10-23).
- **Natt** (`docs/readme/night.jpg`): a black page with a dim clock, the next wakeup ("Väckning 08:45", "Väckning mån 08:45", "Snooze till 08:50" or "Ingen väckning": `sensor.jarvis_nasta_vackning`'s `panel` attribute, 2026-11-02), the timer and the next reminder. It starts with the Natt tab, at **Natt börjar**, or after two minutes without a touch between the two times; a touch or **Natt slutar** ends it. The screen goes to **Nattljus** and the voice to at most 35 %. The three are settings on the panel's device page in Home Assistant (22:00, 06:00 and 12 % at first), kept on the panel so they hold when Home Assistant is down, and Jarvis's quiet hours follow the same two times.
- **The voice page** with the orb, the only place the conversation shows while it goes on. "Hey Jarvis" opens it from any page, and announcements from Jarvis (reminders, alarms) do too. Afterwards it goes back to the page it came from (Natt at night), and a failed question shows why for a moment. Under the orb the screen shows what Jarvis says before his tools ("Genast. Jag kollar …") while he works, then his reply, whole when it fits and otherwise its first sentences. A reply with details (an outing, a plane) shows one or two cards instead, one per subject, each a title and rows of an icon and one line, at most six lines (`docs/readme/answer.jpg`, `python3 jarvis_orb.py --orb --card`). Jarvis writes the cards himself with the tool `skarm` and picks the icons from 110 Material Design Icons by what each row is about; he still speaks the reply.

Timers are Home Assistant's own ("sätt en timer på tio minuter"). The panel counts down the shortest one and chimes every 4 s for a minute when it is done (`assets/sounds/timer.wav`, made by `make_timer.py`); a touch or "Hey Jarvis" stops it. Stills: `python3 jarvis_orb.py --page hemmet|natt|tavla` in `styles/glass-v2`.

To click around it on the Mac as the board will look, with today's NASA picture from Jarvis (the simulator downloads the same images as the board, and Tavla's plaque gets today's title from `apod.json`; the rest shows example values, since the simulator has no Home Assistant):

```sh
tools/sim.sh                        # or --page installningar|hemmet|natt|tavla, --orb --card, --local
```

which does this:

```sh
cd styles/glass-v2
python3 jarvis_orb.py --remote http://jarvis.local:8123/local/hallpanel > jarvis-orb.yaml
../../.venv/bin/esphome compile jarvis-orb.yaml
.esphome/build/style-glass-jarvis/.pioenvs/style-glass-jarvis/program
```

To click through every background in Inställningar's Bakgrund list without changing Joel's choice on Jarvis: `tools/sim.sh --backgrounds` (add `--page installningar` to open on it). `tools/sim_backgrounds.py` copies Jarvis's pictures read only, publishes each choice into its own folder and serves them on 127.0.0.1:8799; `jarvis_orb.py` fills the list from their `choices.json` (tests/test_sim_backgrounds.py).

## LVGL notes

- Every character on screen must be in `GLYPHS` (or `PLAQUE_GLYPHS` for the plaque and music fonts) in `styles/glass-v2/gen.py`. A missing character shows up as a box.
- LVGL cannot blur behind the cards cheaply. Instead, the glass is baked into the background image (`bake.py`).
- One 1024×600 image in RGB565 takes 1.2 MB.
- Add a new widget after its existing siblings when the order does not matter: an insert shifts the tree path of every later sibling, and the visual gate fails on the moved anchor (Natt's `n_wake` before the full-screen tap, 2026-11-02; docs/visual-regression.md). Over a full-screen tap, give it `clickable: False` so the tap still reaches what lies under it.

The other style experiments (futuristic, frameless, glass, clean, hologram, four voice screens) and the first
`gen.py` prototype were removed on 2026-10-30; they are in git history before that date.
